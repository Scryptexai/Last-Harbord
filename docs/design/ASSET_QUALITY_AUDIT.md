## External Asset Limitation
Due to network security restrictions (outbound TLS isolation network policies), direct automated downloads from external CDNs (like Poly Haven, ambientCG, Kenney) were unsuccessful. 

As a result:
- No externally sourced assets were acquired.
- All current 44 models are procedurally generated stand-ins.
- These assets must be replaced with handcrafted production assets before the final world migration.

## Assets Requiring Manual Download (Priority List)
The following asset categories remain highly critical for the visual quality of the project and should be sourced manually by a human artist/developer from authorized marketplace sources, then integrated into `assets/environment/`:

1. **Tropical vegetation**: Hero palm trees, broadleaf trees, tropical ground foliage.
2. **Hero trees**: Complex organic models replacing the mathematical procedural forms.
3. **Survivor buildings**: Professionally modeled cabin and shed kits.
4. **Dock**: Handcrafted wooden dock components showing believable wear and weathering.
5. **Boat**: A survival boat model featuring organic topology and logical wear details.
6. **Large rocks**: Photoscanned or expertly sculpted boulders for silhouettes.
7. **Environmental hero props**: Storage containers, lanterns, distinct debris structures.
